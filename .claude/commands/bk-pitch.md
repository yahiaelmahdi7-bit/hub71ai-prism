Build the Bankable pitch kit from what is actually built.

1. Make sure docs/STATUS.md is current (what is in, what is cut). The writers describe only what is in.
2. In ONE message, launch in parallel (separate lanes: docs/demo, docs/pitch, docs/qna):
   - `bankable-demo-director`: 3-min finals script, 90-s first-round script, backup plan, rehearsal checklist.
   - `bankable-pitch-writer`: submission text, spoken 3-min pitch, taglines, in Yahia's voice (yahia-voice professional, then humanizer).
   - `bankable-qna-coach`: 12 judge questions with honest 1-2 sentence answers, grounded in the licence memo.
3. When all three return, launch `bankable-truth-auditor` on docs/demo, docs/pitch and docs/qna. HOLD means the owning writer fixes the exact lines and the auditor re-checks; repeat once.
4. Report to Yahia in 3-6 lines, plain English, verdict first: kit ready (PASS) or what is being fixed, where the files are, and what to rehearse first.

Rules: no traction, partner, licence or user claims; writers edit only their own docs folder; foreground only; no pushing.
